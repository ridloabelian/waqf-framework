## 13 Prophetic Hadith Protocols Compliance Checklist

### Pre-Flight Gate (`W`, `A`, & `Q` Readiness — Must Pass Before Execution)
- [ ] **P01 · *Innamal A'malu bin-Niyyat*:** Transcendental purpose and real *Maslahah* documented.
- [ ] **P02 · *Al-Bisyarah*:** Compelling strategic horizon shared across the team.
- [ ] **P03 · *Al-Miqdar*:** 1–3 bounded targets, risk ceilings, **Idiot Index** RAB audit, and **Single-Fire** (>=80% focus on 1 bottleneck) locked.
- [ ] **P04 · *Hubab bin Mundzir*:** 100% *Tauqifi* separated from >=70% *Ijtihadi*, and >=20–30% useless bureaucracy deleted before ERP/AI automation.
- [ ] **P05 · *Badr*:** Timeboxed *Syura* (`P1 < 30m`, `P0 < 2h`) ratified shared `'Azam` with an explicit **Kill / Rollback Trigger**.
- [ ] **P06 · *Abu Dzar Al-Ghifari*:** Every DRI cleared *Al-Amanah* & *Al-Quwwah* (**1-Question Truth Test** down to microscopic failure modes); rotations handled with dignity.
- [ ] **P07 · *Al-Itqan*:** Definition of Done, **Goldilocks `+4%`** stretch, and **>=80% Signal-to-Noise** attached to deliverables.
- [ ] **P08 · *Al-Uswah Al-Hasanah*:** Frontline bottleneck co-location points scheduled.
- [ ] **P09 · *Fa-a'inuhum*:** Fastest **skip-level** unblocking path, **La Yuqallad** floor (`>=6h` sleep, no extreme overwork, zero humiliation), and H+0 pay locked.

### Mid-Cycle & Post-Flight Gate (`F · Fahs` — Must Pass at Review)
- [ ] **P10 · *Hisbah / Taftish*:** Mid-cycle inspection AND **Proactive External Negative Feedback Hunting** completed.
- [ ] **P11 · *At-Tahqiq (Two Youths of Badr)*:** Every KPI claim backed by verifiable physical or digital artifacts.
- [ ] **P12 · *Ibnul Lutbiyyah*:** Outcome variance audited AND anti-gratification / asset-segregation audit marked **CLEAN**.
- [ ] **P13 · *Ta'ridh & Bani Makhzum*:** Technical errors converted into dignified *Islah*; new business expansions cleared **Semantic Tree (`5-Hour Trunk`)**; integrity breaches enforced with zero elite impunity.
