## 13 Prophetic Hadith Protocols Compliance Checklist

### Pre-Flight Gate (Must Pass Before Execution)
- [ ] **P01 · *Innamal A'malu bin-Niyyat*:** Transcendental purpose and real *Maslahah* documented.
- [ ] **P02 · *Al-Bisyarah*:** Compelling strategic horizon shared across the team.
- [ ] **P03 · *Al-Miqdar*:** 1–3 bounded numerical targets, firm deadlines, and risk ceilings defined.
- [ ] **P04 · *Hubab bin Mundzir*:** *Tauqifi* (fixed) vs. *Ijtihadi* (open to critique) boundaries explicitly separated.
- [ ] **P05 · *Badr*:** Tactical plan co-ratified in *Syura* as shared `'Azam`.
- [ ] **P06 · *Abu Dzar Al-Ghifari*:** Every DRI/PIC cleared *Al-Quwwah* & *Al-Amanah*; any declines/rotations handled with psychological safety.
- [ ] **P07 · *Al-Itqan*:** Explicit Definition of Done (DoD) attached to core deliverables.
- [ ] **P08 · *Al-Uswah Al-Hasanah*:** Frontline leadership engagement points scheduled.
- [ ] **P09 · *Fa-a'inuhum*:** Manager unblocking plan, zero-humiliation standard, and punctual payment schedule locked.

### Mid-Cycle & Post-Flight Gate (Must Pass at Review)
- [ ] **P10 · *Hisbah / Taftish*:** Mid-cycle process and ethics inspection completed.
- [ ] **P11 · *At-Tahqiq (Two Youths of Badr)*:** Every KPI claim backed by verifiable physical or digital artifacts.
- [ ] **P12 · *Ibnul Lutbiyyah*:** Outcome variance audited AND anti-gratification / asset-segregation audit marked **CLEAN**.
- [ ] **P13 · *Ta'ridh & Bani Makhzum*:** Technical errors converted into dignified systemic *Islah*; integrity breaches enforced equally with zero executive impunity.
